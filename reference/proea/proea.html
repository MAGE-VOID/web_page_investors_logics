10 QUESTIONS
What exactly do I get when I buy?+

A download, not a login. Your email unlocks the member area right after checkout: the product zip with full source (MQL5 for the EA, Pine v6 for the indicators), setup guides, and AI-ready docs. The files live on your machine and stay yours.

Do I need a subscription?+

No. Every product is a one-time purchase with lifetime updates included. There is no monthly fee and nothing to cancel.

Is the 372× backtest number real?+

It is a modelled illustration, and we are precise about what that means: MT5 Strategy Tester with generated ticks (Model=0), 29 months, $10,000 to $3.72M, with a 73% max drawdown along the way. It is not reproducible — the same config has produced 166×–575× across broker data snapshots, and we publish that spread. Not live performance, not a promise. What you keep is the source and the research.

What do I need to run the tools?+

MetaTrader 5 with any broker that allows expert advisors for the EA. A free TradingView plan is enough for the indicators. TradeLens is a local journal that runs from files you own. No VPS is required to start.

I'm new to automation — is this too advanced for me?+

The stack is built as a path, not a cliff. Start by running the documented EA or charting with the indicators, then grow into journaling and building your own systems. The guides assume no coding background, and the AI-ready docs are written so your AI assistant can walk you through every step.

Can I read the code before trusting it?+

Yes — that is the point. Every product ships as readable source: entry logic, risk rules, everything is inspectable. Nothing is a locked black box.

Can I use these for prop-firm challenges?+

The Prop Firm Challenge Toolkit exists exactly for that — it turns challenge rules into live dollar numbers on your chart. The journal and indicators work with prop accounts too. Always check your firm's own EA policy before automating a funded account.

How do updates work?+

Lifetime updates are included with every purchase. When a product ships a new version, it appears in your member area at no extra cost.

How does the refund work?+

Email support within 7 days of purchase, before you download the files, and we refund in full — one email, no forms. Once the source is downloaded it is delivered and the window closes; a corrupt or incomplete download is refunded or replaced at any time.

Is this a signal service?+

No. We never call trades. You buy tools, source code and research that you own, and learn to run your own systems. Trading carries a substantial risk of loss — never trade money you cannot afford to lose.
