# Software Edition — source-only finish review

Historical record of the initial Software Edition build. A later user-directed refinement softened the hierarchy and introduced separate purchase and rental enquiries; see the current surface brief and DESIGN.md for that implementation. This initial review does not certify the later code.

## Scope

Complete frontend redesign: home, navigation, product and license selection, email request, documentation, support and 404. The user authorized subagents and generated imagery, but excluded terminal commands, ports and browser use. No application, build or tests were run.

The independent finish reviewer inspected composition B, the production product image, the design contract and the selected implementation sources. This is not a browser screenshot review or a claim of runtime verification.

## Deliberate translations

- Composition B was selected under the user's earlier delegation to propose the direction, not represented as a new explicit visual approval.
- The redundant second product close-up in the mock was omitted to simplify the commercial story.
- Pricing remains dark to preserve the requested dark-mode identity; porcelain is used for product explanation and reading.
- Mock BUY labels and speculative copy were replaced by accurate license-request wording.
- The package is explicitly labeled as an illustration of digital software. It does not imply a physical product.

## Preserve in future work

Keep the product portrait prominent; the three license periods comparable; native radio inputs, focus treatments and reduced-motion support intact; and the distinction between a draft email and an implemented purchase flow explicit.
