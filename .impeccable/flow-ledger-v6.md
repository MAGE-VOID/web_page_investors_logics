- **Mismatch:** the dark commercial system was being rendered as oversized editorial typography with too many technical markers.
  **Fix:** introduced shared hero/section/title/body tokens, reduced route scales, and removed decorative preview codes and evidence/capability numbers.

  **Fix:** kept graphite and night fields, reserved champagne for action/selection, and changed risk callouts to restrained outlined fields.
